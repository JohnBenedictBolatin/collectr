# Library and shelves

- **Job:** Help a private collector add, group, sort, search, and physically place a mixed collection of dozens to hundreds of individual copies.
- **Primary action:** Add a collectible. Success means the new item appears in the library, can join named collections, and can be assigned to a cabinet row and slot.
- **Surface direction:** The approved “Catalog Desk” pairs a searchable sortable catalog with compact shelf previews and a recoverable unplaced-items route. See `.impeccable/mocks/desktop-library-approved.png`.
- **Layout and interaction:** Desktop navigation rail, readable catalog workspace, quick shelf glance. Mobile library uses a 2-column tile grid and bottom navigation. Cabinet slots support drag, keyboard, and touch controls.
- **Scope and states:** Private signed-in workspace; email/password account, empty, loading, saving, upload failure, occupied slot, offline loss of connection, long labels, and multi-session conflicts. English; responsive desktop and mobile; reduce motion.
- **Boundary:** No public profiles, external item catalog, sales, market prices, or 3D room in the first release.
