# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with TypeScript, Supabase Auth/Postgres/Storage, and Vercel hosting (selected by delegation for account-based web, private photo storage, and later marketplace extension).

## Users

Private individual collectors of mixed hobbies such as vinyl figures, action figures, and trading cards. They want to catalog what they own, group items by interest, and remember where each item is displayed or stored.

## Product Purpose

Provide a personal online home where collectors can manage their inventory and arrange their items on visual shelf displays. First-release success means a collector can add, group, place, and later find their collectibles across devices.

## Positioning

Connect each cataloged collectible to both its interest-based collections and its physical shelf location, so the library and display describe the same owned items.

## Operating Context

Collectors manage collections ranging from dozens to hundreds of mixed items. They may own multiple copies. They add items manually and may attach personal photographs. They organize named collections independently from cabinets, rows, and shelf slots.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- First release is private and account-based, with email/password authentication and verified email.
- Initial categories: Funko Pop, Action Figure, Card, and Other. No external product catalog is required.
- One item record represents one physical copy. An item can belong to multiple named collections and at most one shelf slot.
- Cabinets contain configurable rows and slots. Shelves are visual organizers, not dimensionally accurate fit planners.
- Item photos are private. Public profiles, marketplace listings, valuation, CSV import, and 3D displays are deferred.
- English, light theme, online use, and desktop plus mobile web are the initial defaults.
- Public product naming is undecided; “Collectr” is a working name only.

## Brand Commitments

The user selected a calm neobrutalist visual identity with a maximum of five authored interface colors. Keep warm ivory `#F7F3E8` and near-black `#20201E`; use teal `#78C5AA`, orange `#EC781C`, and golden yellow `#F4A32A` for colored surfaces and accents. Do not use the earlier lilac or sage proposal. Cole is the proposed original black-cat superhero mascot, with ivory facial details and a gold or orange emblem; recolor his cape with the approved accent palette. Preferred tagline: “Your collection. In its place.”

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied five neobrutalist design reference images. No production product catalog, real collector content, brand assets, or testimonials were supplied. Use clearly labeled sample collection entries in demonstrations.

## Product Principles

- Make adding and finding a collectible quick.
- Keep personal ownership and photographs private by default.
- Keep collection membership independent from physical placement.
- Make the shelf a useful, accessible view of owned items.
- Keep future public sharing opt-in and separate from private collection data.
